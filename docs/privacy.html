Flevora Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • App: Flevora ("Share Your World.")

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Info: Email address, username, full name, bio, website, and profile photo via Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore.
  • User Content: Photos, videos (Reels), 24-hour Stories, captions, location tags, comments, likes, and direct messages.
  • Device & Notifications: Expo Push Notification token (used only to deliver likes, comments, follows, and message alerts).

2. Third-Party Services

Flevora uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) for account and social data, and Cloudinary for hosting uploaded images and videos. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Safety, Moderation & Age

Flevora is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users can block other accounts, switch to a Private Account, hide posts, and report abusive content or users. Moderators review reports to keep the community safe.

4. Data Export & Account Deletion

You can download your data or permanently delete your account inside the app under Settings → Delete Account, or online anytime using our Web Account Deletion Page.

Deleting your account removes your Firebase Authentication account, profile document, posts, stories, and notifications.
